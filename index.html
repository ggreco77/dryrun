Telescope & Field of View

Positive PA rotates from North toward West (current GRAWITA convention). One FoV for manual pointing, the classic strategies and GW Tiling (rectangles only). New FoVs use it; accepted FoVs keep their own geometry.

GW Tiling · contiguous FoVs over the connected zones of the GW credible region

Web Worker · MOCWasm
?

The FoV (rectangles only), its size and position angle come from Telescope & Field of View. Set one overlap: 0 % means that each new FoV touches an earlier one along an edge or at a vertex; above 0 % it is the share of the area of the FoV it is attached to. Choose the 10, 50 or 90 % credible region and its zones (connected parts of the region, North or South only when the whole zone lies on that side of the celestial equator), and optionally up to three MOCs of the left panel to intersect with. Generate coverage plans up to 20 contiguous FoVs in a Web Worker, targets in decreasing GW probability; Cancel stops it and leaves the plan unchanged. A branch is contiguous to an earlier FoV (the telescope moves back), a transfer moves to the next target. Probabilities are fractions of the whole BAYESTAR map, never renormalised; the official P(FoV), P(new) and P(cum) are those of the observation sequence.

FoV shape, size and PA: Telescope & Field of View (rectangles only). Overlap 0 %: every new FoV touches an earlier FoV along an edge or at a vertex. Above 0 %: the area it shares with the FoV it is attached to is that percentage of that FoV's area (accepted up to 1 point more).
